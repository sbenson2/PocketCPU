Privacy and data handling.

These notes describe the website and current development build. They will be reviewed again before public app distribution.

This website

The website has no analytics scripts, advertising trackers, signup forms, third-party fonts, or PocketCPU account system. Its assets are served by GitHub Pages.

GitHub may process visitor IP addresses and request information to operate the hosting service. See GitHub's privacy statement. Opening an external link is subject to that site's practices.

On your device

The app stores a host address, device identity, saved tabs, and drafts. Device access credentials are kept in the device-only iOS Keychain. Connection errors do not deliberately clear saved work.

Closing a tab removes that local tab and its draft. It does not delete the underlying OpenCode conversation on your computer.

On your computer

The companion stores configured project locations and pairing/device records in a protected state file. Pairing codes and device tokens are stored as hashes. The host owner can revoke a device locally; revocation blocks access and does not erase the host's session history.

OpenCode manages coding sessions on your computer. Its model configuration determines whether prompts, code, and tool output are sent to a model provider. Those providers have their own terms, retention, and privacy practices.

During a connection

The app uses HTTPS with system certificate validation. Direct connections go to the host endpoint the user selects. Choosing Tailscale also involves that network service and its policies.

The experimental relay terminates TLS and can read forwarded credentials and session traffic in memory. It is not end-to-end encrypted. There is no PocketCPU-operated public relay in this development release.

Support and reports

Public GitHub issues are visible to others. Do not post passwords, pairing codes, tokens, private source code, or personal information. Use the repository's security reporting guidance for vulnerabilities.

Final retention/removal instructions, App Store privacy disclosures, and the release privacy policy remain part of launch preparation. No company account or payment information is collected by this website.
