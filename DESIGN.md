# Design notes

Reading this as: a product landing page for mobile developers, with native Apple clarity and a portable-computer feel, leaning toward quiet product documentation.

References: [Apple Terminal guide](https://support.apple.com/guide/terminal/welcome/mac), [Panic Prompt](https://panic.com/prompt/), and [Apple Human Interface Guidelines](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/). These inform restrained system typography, generous spacing, and device-led evidence without copying their UI.

Tokens: cool paper `#f2f6f8`, ink `#0f2138`, teal `#006e78`, white `#ffffff`, gray-blue text `#526579`, and rules `#cbd7dc`. System/Avenir Next fonts keep the site native-adjacent; screenshots carry product detail. Mobile layout collapses at 680px, wider composition settles at 900px. Dark mode maps the same tokens to ink-blue surfaces. Motion is limited to smooth scrolling and respects reduced-motion preferences.

The screenshots are actual iOS simulator captures. The visible sample labels must remain: these images demonstrate the development interface, not a live coding result. No CPTR, Apple, or other product artwork is included.
