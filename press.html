PocketCPU press kit.

A native iPhone and iPad client for OpenCode sessions running on your own computer.

Product facts

Status
In development. No public App Store release yet.
Audience
Developers who want to check and continue AI coding sessions from their phone or iPad.
Client
Native SwiftUI app for iPhone and iPad, requiring iOS/iPadOS 17 or later.
Current workflow
Pairing, configured projects, text sessions, send/stop, persistent tabs, and drafts.
Companion
Self-hosted command-line development builds for macOS and Linux. Native Windows and consumer desktop installation are planned.
Planned price
US$10 once for lifetime app access, with no PocketCPU subscription and a free companion. Model-provider and hosting costs remain separate.
Connections
Direct HTTPS, optional Tailscale HTTPS, and an experimental self-hosted relay. The relay is not end-to-end encrypted.

Short description

PocketCPU is a native iPhone and iPad client for OpenCode on a user's own computer. Developers can resume text conversations, send or stop requests, and keep local tabs and drafts. The project is in development; its planned price is US$10 once, with a free self-hosted companion.
